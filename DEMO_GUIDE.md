# TrustDoc AI - Demo Guide

## Steps for Demonstration

1. **Login**: Log in as a regular user or admin. Say: *"First, we access the secure portal."*
2. **Upload**: Upload a sample document. Say: *"We upload a degree certificate for verification."*
3. **AI Pipeline (OCR & Forensics)**: Click "Analyze". Say: *"The system is now running OCR extraction and Photoshop detection."*
4. **View Extraction**: Show the extracted data and the Forensic Risk Score. Say: *"As you can see, it automatically pulled the name and gave it a high trust score because the image is untampered."*
5. **Cross-Document Check**: Upload a second document with a different DOB. Run analysis. Say: *"The system detected an inconsistency between the Marksheet and Degree."*
6. **Registration**: Log in as Admin, go to Review, and Approve the document. Then Register it. Say: *"The document is now immutably registered."*
7. **QR Scan**: Open the generated QR code on the screen and scan it with your phone. Say: *"Anyone can scan this to instantly verify the document's authenticity without an account."*
8. **Revocation**: Go back to Admin, Revoke the document. Scan the QR again. Say: *"The QR now instantly shows the document is REVOKED, preventing fraud."*
